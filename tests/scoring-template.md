# Scoring Template — Artificial Wisdom Test Suite

For each test prompt, score the agent's response on 8 dimensions (1-5).
Score WITHOUT the skill first (baseline), then WITH the skill (treatment).

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purpose fit | Misses the real need | Answers what was asked | Solves the deeper objective |
| 2 | Context fit | Ignores constraints | Acknowledges context | Works within and around constraints |
| 3 | Truthfulness | Fabrications present | Factually accurate | Grounded with caveats |
| 4 | Usefulness | Abstract, needs translation | Actionable with effort | Immediately executable |
| 5 | Risk awareness | Ignores or hides risks | Mentions major risks | Surfaces with mitigation |
| 6 | Proportionality | Overbuilt or underbuilt | Appropriate depth | Exactly right |
| 7 | Human agency | Removes human from decisions | Presents options | Frames the decision, recommends, defers |
| 8 | Taste | Jargon, sludge, AI-isms | Clear and professional | Memorable, human-feeling |
