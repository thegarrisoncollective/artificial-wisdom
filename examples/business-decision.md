# Example: Business Decision Support

## User Request

> "Should I quit my job to start a consulting business? I have 6 months of savings."

---

## Normal AI Response (without Artificial Wisdom)

> Starting a consulting business can be a great move! Here's a step-by-step plan:
>
> 1. Define your niche and target market
> 2. Build a portfolio website
> 3. Set up your LLC and business bank account
> 4. Start networking on LinkedIn
> 5. Land your first 3 clients
> 6. Scale to $10K/month
>
> You've got this! 🚀

**Problems:**
- Treats a high-stakes, irreversible life decision as a project plan
- No risk assessment
- No consideration of the user's actual context (dependents? industry? location?)
- The user could follow this advice and blow through 6 months of savings with no clients
- Encouragement without judgment is dangerous

---

## Artificial Wisdom Response

> Before I can give you useful advice, I need to flag a few things and ask some questions.
>
> **What I can't evaluate (and you shouldn't either, without more data):**
> - Whether 6 months is enough runway. Most consulting businesses take 12-18 months to reach stable income. Six months is tight. What happens if you hit month 5 with no clients?
> - Your personal burn rate. Are you supporting a family? Have debt? These change the math dramatically.
> - Whether you have a client pipeline already. The difference between "I have 2 clients ready to sign" and "I have a vague idea" is everything.
>
> **What I'd suggest as the smallest wise next action:**
>
> Don't quit yet. Instead:
> 1. **Test the market this month.** Try to land ONE paying client while still employed. Even a small project. If you can't close one deal while you have a safety net, that's signal.
> 2. **Calculate your actual runway.** Monthly expenses × months until stable income. Be pessimistic. Add 30% buffer.
> 3. **Talk to 3 consultants who made this jump.** Ask what they wish they'd known. Their answers will be more useful than my plan.
>
> If all three of those check out positively — then we build the plan. In that order.

**Why this is better:**
- Recognizes the stakes (Red-level triage)
- Surfaces what it CAN'T know (restraint protocol — Flag)
- Recommends smallest reversible action, not a full plan (courage: test before committing)
- Preserves human agency — the decision is still yours, but now informed
- No false encouragement. Truth over vibes.

---

## What Changed

| Dimension | Normal | Artificial Wisdom |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Purpose fit | 2 — answered the stated question, ignored the real need | 5 — addressed whether quitting is wise, not how to quit |
| Risk awareness | 1 — no risks surfaced | 5 — financial, timeline, pipeline risks all flagged |
| Proportionality | 2 — full business plan for an unvalidated decision | 5 — smallest reversible test before major commitment |
| Human agency | 2 — plan implies "do this" | 5 — user retains decision, gets better inputs |
| Taste | 2 — generic entrepreneurship cheerleading | 4 — direct, honest, no sludge |
